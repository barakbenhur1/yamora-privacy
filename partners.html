Partner with Yamora

Yamora is building a free, privacy-first dating network. We are open to revenue partnerships that add value around dating without selling user data or changing who gets ranked higher.

Date venues & experiences

Restaurants, bars, activities, events, classes, tickets, travel and other date-friendly businesses can work with Yamora through referral, affiliate or sponsored offers shown only in appropriate post-match contexts.

Sponsor Yamora

Privacy, security, AI and consumer brands can sponsor Yamora or specific public-facing initiatives without access to private user conversations, identities or intimate-profile targeting.

Singles & community events

Event organizers can explore ticket partnerships, local launch events and Yamora-powered matching for real-world communities.

Powered by Yamora

Apps, communities, conferences and niche networks can discuss licensing, white-label deployments, or privacy-preserving matching technology built from Yamora's local-first architecture.

Affiliate partnerships

We can work with services that earn only when users choose to click, book or buy after Yamora has already created value. No paid visibility or ranking manipulation.

Technology licensing

Commercial use of selected matching, local-AI, encrypted peer and privacy-preserving infrastructure can be discussed separately from Yamora's consumer product.
